03. esp32-rfid.ino
IoT • Hardware
images/project-rfid.jpg 1280 × 720 px
IoT • Hardware Project

Smart RFID Attendance System

A classroom attendance terminal: students tap their RFID card, the ESP32 checks the card's UID and sends the record over Wi-Fi to a Supabase database in real time. An LCD, green and red LEDs, and a buzzer confirm every scan.

ESP32 RC522 RFID Supabase Wi-Fi + HTTP I2C LCD
GitHub
Details
  • Hardware: ESP32, RC522 RFID reader, 16×2 I2C LCD, green and red LEDs, and a buzzer, wired on a breadboard and housed in a custom enclosure.
  • Firmware: Arduino libraries WiFi, MFRC522, SPI, LiquidCrystal I2C, and HTTPClient. Reads each card's UID, validates it, and posts the attendance record to Supabase over HTTP.
  • Feedback: The LCD shows "Tap RFID Card" and the scan result. Green LED and a short beep mean success; red LED and an alert mean an invalid card.
  • Problems solved: Fixed a blank LCD caused by swapped SDA/SCL wiring, fixed the RFID reader not reading cards by correcting the SPI pin assignments, and added Wi-Fi reconnection logic for unstable connections.
